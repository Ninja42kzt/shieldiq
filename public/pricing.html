Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a credit card for the free plan? +
No. The free plan requires no payment details. Just register and start training immediately.
What happens after my 7-day trial ends? +
Business and Enterprise trials last 7 days. Individual and Individual Pro trials last 3 days. When a trial ends, your subscription starts unless you cancel before then, and you will get a reminder email before it ends.
How does the AI feature work? +
The AI analyzes each team member's quiz results, identifies their weak areas, and generates personalized training recommendations. It's available on the Business, Enterprise, Individual and Individual Pro plans.
Can I add more than 10 users on Business? +
Business supports up to 10 users at $5/user/month (max $50/month). Enterprise supports up to 30 users at $8/user/month (max $240/month). Need more than that? Contact us for a custom plan.
Is payment secure? +
Yes. All payments are processed securely through PayPal. Nothing is charged until your free trial ends, and you can cancel anytime before then.
